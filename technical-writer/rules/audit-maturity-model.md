## Why It Matters

The maturity model provides a roadmap —
not a judgment.
It helps teams understand where they are and what the next achievable level looks like.
Adapted from Google OpenDocs Content Maturity Checklist with enterprise extensions.

## The Four Levels

Use the canonical
[Documentation Maturity Model checklist](../references/enterprise-patterns.md#documentation-maturity-model)
for the requirements at each level:

- **Seeds**: Readers can find the product, install it, and try an example.
- **Foundation**: Readers can complete core tasks using organized guides and reference.
- **Integration**: Teams maintain documentation through their development workflow.
- **Excellence**: Teams use research and quality metrics to improve adoption.

## How to Use

For Foundation, verify that the quickstart takes under 10 minutes.

1. Assess current level honestly — partial completion of a level means you're still at the previous level
2. Focus on completing the current level before advancing
3. Use the checklist items as sprint tasks
4. Reassess quarterly
